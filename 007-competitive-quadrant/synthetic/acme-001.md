## Competitor: Acme WorkOS

### Top 5 recurring complaints
1. **Onboarding takes weeks for non-technical teams** — Despite marketing claims of "5-minute setup," admins report spending 2–3 weeks configuring integrations and permissions before teams can actually use the product.
2. **AI suggestions are generic and miss industry context** — The AI copilot surfaces recommendations that feel copy-pasted rather than tailored to the user's vertical; sales teams in healthcare and finance find it nearly useless without heavy prompt engineering.
3. **Reporting dashboard lags behind real-time activity by up to 2 hours** — Revenue operations leaders can't trust the numbers for live pipeline reviews, forcing them to pull from secondary sources.
4. **Mobile experience is read-only in practice** — While technically functional on mobile, any action beyond reading a record requires multiple taps and frequent session timeouts.
5. **Customer support response times are slow for non-enterprise tiers** — Mid-market customers report waiting 3–5 business days for critical bug escalations.

### Top 5 recurring praise points
1. **Deep CRM-to-billing integration saves significant reconciliation time** — Finance and RevOps teams cite the native billing sync as a 10–15 hour per month time save versus stitching together Salesforce and Stripe.
2. **Customisable pipeline stages that mirror real sales motions** — Unlike rigid competitors, Acme lets teams define their own stages with conditional logic, which sales managers say reduces "stage inflation" and improves forecast accuracy.
3. **AI-powered call summaries land in the CRM automatically** — Reps love that they never have to type notes after discovery calls; the summaries are detailed enough that managers can coach from them.
4. **Transparent usage-based pricing is easy to justify internally** — Finance approvers rarely push back on renewals because the cost scales predictably with actual seats and API calls.
5. **Strong Slack integration keeps deal updates visible without switching tools** — Deal room notifications in Slack channels reduce the meeting overhead of weekly pipeline reviews.

### Exact customer language
> "We replaced three separate tools with Acme and our RevOps team finally stopped fighting over which spreadsheet was the source of truth."

> "The call summaries are so good that my manager coaches me on calls I wasn't even on."

> "Setup wasn't as fast as they said, but once it clicked everything just worked together."
