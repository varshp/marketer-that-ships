## Competitor: Drift Engage

### Core pitch
Drift Engage markets itself as the real-time buyer engagement platform — converting anonymous website visitors into pipeline through AI-powered conversational experiences timed to the buyer's exact intent signal.

### Who they're targeting
- **Demand generation and growth marketing teams** at B2B SaaS companies with high-traffic websites and low conversion rates.
- **Companies investing in ABM** who want to personalise web experiences for named accounts without developer involvement.
- **Sales development leaders** looking to eliminate cold outreach by replacing it with warm, in-moment conversations.

### Top 3 positioning bets
1. **Real-time intent + real-time conversation.** Drift's key bet is that the moment of peak intent (when a visitor is on the pricing page for the third time this week) is a narrow window, and only an AI agent — not a human SDR who picks up the lead in 4 hours — can capture it. Their data claims 7x higher conversion for conversations initiated within 5 seconds of a high-intent signal.
2. **Personalisation without engineering.** Their audience targeting builder lets marketing segment visitors by firmographic, technographic, and behavioural signals and serve different playbooks — without writing a line of code. Positioned against CMS-heavy personalisation projects that take quarters.
3. **Revenue team alignment through shared playbooks.** Drift claims to close the handoff gap between marketing, SDR, and AE by putting every interaction — chat, email follow-up, meeting booked — in a single timeline visible to the full revenue team.

### What they're not saying
- Their AI conversations still frequently route incorrectly, frustrating visitors and creating negative brand impressions at the highest-intent moments.
- Customers in low-traffic segments (<10k monthly visitors) rarely see ROI from the platform's cost.
- Sales and CS teams report the unified timeline is only as clean as the CRM sync, which has known data duplication issues.

### Exact customer language
> "We used to lose deals because an SDR called back four hours too late. Now the AI is there in 10 seconds and the prospect is already in a meeting by the time our rep wakes up."

> "I can personalise 40 different audience segments without filing a single engineering ticket. That alone is worth the cost."
