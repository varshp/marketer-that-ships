## Competitor: Bridge AI

### Core pitch
Bridge positions itself as the AI-native GTM platform for PLG companies — converting product usage signals directly into pipeline actions without human SDR involvement.

### Who they're targeting
- **Growth-stage SaaS companies** (50–500 employees) running product-led growth motions who want to layer sales-assist on top of self-serve.
- **Revenue teams frustrated by MQL-based funnels** that reward form fills over actual product engagement.
- **Head of Growth and VP Sales roles** who want a unified view of product-qualified accounts.

### Top 3 positioning bets
1. **Product-qualified leads over marketing-qualified leads.** Bridge bets that usage signals (feature activation, session frequency, collaboration invites) are stronger buying signals than downloaded whitepapers. All scoring is built around product telemetry.
2. **AI that writes the outreach, not just surfaces the account.** Their differentiator is a "signal-to-sequence" engine that auto-generates personalised outreach based on exactly which features the prospect has and hasn't activated — cutting SDR research time by ~70% per their case studies.
3. **No-code signal builder for non-technical GTM teams.** Rather than forcing RevOps to write SQL or work through an engineer, Bridge provides a drag-and-drop signal composer. They claim 80% of customers configure their first PQL model without engineering help.

### What they're not saying
- Their AI outreach quality degrades quickly for accounts outside their training data (primarily B2B SaaS); customers in industrial or healthcare verticals report near-useless suggestions.
- The platform requires clean product analytics data (Amplitude, Mixpanel, Segment) — customers without mature instrumentation cannot use core features.
- Enterprise security reviews regularly flag their data handling practices; SOC 2 Type II was only achieved in Q1 2025.

### Exact customer language
> "We went from 8% PQL conversion to 21% in one quarter. The sequence timing is what made the difference — it hits accounts at the exact moment of activation."

> "Our SDRs used to spend half their day researching. Now they just review and approve. It's a completely different job."
