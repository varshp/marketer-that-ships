**Key observations:**

- **Sales dominance is extreme.** At 43% of all open headcount, Notion is in a full-throttle GTM build. This isn't incremental growth—it's a company re-architecting its go-to-market from product-led to sales-led at global scale. The Sales bucket includes not just quota-carriers but also Solutions Engineers (~15 roles), Forward Deployed Engineers (5 roles across 5 countries), Professional Services Consultants (6+ roles), and Partner Managers (5 roles)—a complete enterprise selling apparatus being stood up simultaneously.
- **Geographic spread is remarkable.** Roles span **15 cities across 13 countries**: San Francisco, New York, Dublin, London, Paris, Munich, Amsterdam, Stockholm, Madrid, Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore, Sydney, and Hyderabad. Multiple postings use "founding team" language for Singapore, Sydney, Korea, and several European sub-regions (Benelux, Nordics, Meddit). This is a coordinated global launch, not organic regional trickle.
- **Product Management has exactly 1 open role** (Enterprise PM)—unusually lean for 157 total postings. This signals a mature product org or a deliberate decision to constrain product scope while scaling distribution.
- **Marketing is relatively lean** at ~12 roles, and skews heavily toward regional field/demand gen and enablement rather than core product marketing or brand. Only 2 PMM roles exist, both enterprise-focused.
- **Security is investing specifically in AI security** (Application Security Engineer, AI Security) and enterprise compliance (GRC, Customer Trust), signaling enterprise readiness requirements.

### Inferred strategic priorities

**1. Full-throttle enterprise sales-led motion, globally and simultaneously**

Notion is executing one of the most aggressive PLG-to-enterprise transitions visible in SaaS right now. Evidence:
- 67 Sales-department roles including Enterprise AEs in 10+ countries, many as "first hire" in-region
- Explicit language: *"Notion is transitioning from 'start-up' to 'scale-up'"* (Job 10); *"Notion's sales motion is evolving from product‑led to sales‑led"* (Job 47)
- Complete enterprise selling infrastructure being built simultaneously: Solutions Engineers, Forward Deployed Engineers, Professional Services Consultants, Deal Desk, Partner ecosystem, Onboarding Specialists
- Enterprise Sales Leader role (Job 34) describes *"helping customers reimagine how their organizations collaborate, align, and execute"*—consultative, transformation-grade selling

**2. AI Agents as the enterprise value wedge**

Notion is betting that Custom Agents and agentic AI workflows are the primary driver of enterprise adoption, expansion, and differentiation. Evidence:
- Every Customer Success role (12+) centers on *"AI-powered workflows"* with *"AI at the center"*
- Forward Deployed Engineers (5 roles, 5 countries) are deploying *"production-grade custom agents and AI workflows with MCP, Agent APIs"* directly with enterprise customers
- AI Solutions Specialist role (Job 107) is a *"founding"* position to *"shape how we sell and scale our AI products"*—building the AI GTM motion from scratch
- Enterprise PM role (Job 130) states Notion is *"re-defining what Enterprise-grade means in an agentic world"*
- CS Enablement role (Job 88) is specifically focused on *"AI workflow adoption & utilization"* as the evolution of the CS function

**3. Becoming the IT-approved connected workspace (displacing multiple point solutions)**

Notion is repositioning from a team productivity tool to an enterprise platform that replaces docs, wikis, project management, email, and calendar—and now needs IT buyer approval. Evidence:
- Product description in every posting now includes *"docs, notes, projects, calendar, and email"*—calendar and email are new to the core narrative
- PMM, IT Solutions role (Job 18) specifically targets *"CIOs and Digital Transformation leaders"* with *"enterprise feature launches (analytics, security, permissions, migration)"*
- AI Agentic Search (Job 68) connects across Slack, Google Drive, Salesforce, GitHub—positioning Notion as the search/knowledge layer across the entire enterprise stack
- Solutions Engineers repeatedly reference *"integrate with their existing tech stack, replace existing solutions, or be deployed in large or phased implementation"*
- Content Onboarding engineering team (Job 67) builds *"import and migration experiences"* from competing tools

**4. Building a partner and services ecosystem to scale enterprise delivery**

Notion is constructing a consulting/partner layer to reduce dependency on internal headcount for enterprise implementations. Evidence:
- Two Scaled Partner Manager roles (AMER + EMEA, Jobs 85, 148) describe *"a massive services layer emerge on top of the product—consultants and agencies building workflows, implementations, and repeatable 'operating systems' for customers"*
- Partner Strategy & Operations Manager (Job 89): *"Notion is accelerating investment in Partner and Consultant motions with the goal to scale growth meaningfully over the next several years"*
- Partner Solutions Engineer (Job 122) building a *"Black Belt Program"* to certify partner SEs, targeting *"~20 partner SEs"* by end of 2026
- Partner Sales roles in Japan and APAC suggest reseller/channel strategy for international markets
- Professional Services Consultants being hired in France, DACH, Tokyo, Korea simultaneously

**5. Operationalizing AI internally to gain GTM leverage**

Notion is using AI not just in the product but across internal GTM operations, creating a compound advantage. Evidence:
- GTM AI + Innovation Manager (Job 149): *"architecting and implementing AI-powered solutions that enhance our GTM team's effectiveness"*
- AI Applications Engineer (Job 51): *"strategic partner to internal stakeholders (primarily GTM, Finance and People teams)"* building AI solutions for internal ops
- Finance Business Partner, Workflow Automation (Job 108): *"build scalable planning/reporting workflows with AI-enabled automation"*
- AI Conversation Designer (Job 142): building AI-powered customer support chatbot and email
- Legal Ops role (Job 105) focused on AI enablement for legal operations
- Staff People Analytics (Job 143) building *"AI-native tooling"* for workforce analytics

### PMM actions

**1. Attack the "still building the playbook" window aggressively in international markets.**
Notion explicitly states it is "defining/iterating on sales motions" in most regions and hiring "founding teams" across EMEA sub-regions (Benelux, Nordics, Meddit, MENA), APAC (Singapore, Sydney, Korea), and even enterprise segments in mature markets. Their enterprise sales process is not yet codified. If you compete with Notion, **now is the 6–12 month window to lock in enterprise accounts in these geographies before Notion's sales machine gets traction.** Build region-specific competitive plays, prioritize local-language content and events in DACH, France, Nordics, and Japan, and invest in direct outreach to accounts in Notion's target segment (500–3,000 employees) where your product already has a foothold.

**2. Build and distribute a "Notion AI vs. [Your Product]" competitive narrative before their Enterprise PMM does.**
Notion just posted an Enterprise Product Marketing, GTM role (Job 28) whose explicit mandate is to *"stand up and own Notion's competitor hub"* and build *"battle cards, ROI calculators and business value frameworks, Win/Loss insights."* They are building the competitive apparatus now—it doesn't fully exist yet. **Move first.** Create your own battle cards against Notion AI, Custom Agents, and their agentic search story. Focus on areas where their agentic narrative is ahead of actual enterprise readiness (permissions at scale, data governance, auditability of agent actions). Frame your competitive messaging around enterprise trust, proven-at-scale deployment, and lower switching risk.

**3. Target the IT buyer with a counter-narrative on platform consolidation risk.**
Notion's PMM, IT Solutions role (Job 18) signals a new effort to win CIO and IT decision-maker trust with positioning around *"analytics, security, permissions, migration."* This means Notion is encountering resistance from IT buyers on governance, compliance, and integration maturity. **Position your product (if you're Confluence, Asana, Monday, or similar) on the strengths Notion is still building:** proven enterprise admin controls, mature SIEM/DLP integrations, established compliance certifications, and lower migration risk. Create content aimed at IT leaders that questions the wisdom of consolidating docs + projects + email + calendar + AI agents into a single vendor whose enterprise capabilities are still being stood up.

**4. Exploit the migration motion as a competitive vulnerability.**
Notion has a dedicated Content Onboarding engineering team (Job 67) and Forward Deployed Engineers focused on *"large-scale migrations."* This investment is a signal that migration is hard and high-friction—otherwise they wouldn't need dedicated engineering. **If you're an incumbent being displaced, invest in retention plays:** switching-cost calculators, "why customers stay" case studies, and proactive outreach to customers in the 500–3,000 employee sweet spot. If you're a competitor also targeting Notion's install base, build superior migration tooling and make ease-of-switching a core differentiator in your positioning.**

**5. Develop a credible agentic/AI story or concede the narrative.**
Notion is embedding AI agents into every customer-facing function—Sales, CS, Support, Professional Services, and the product itself. The Custom Agents product, MCP-based integrations, and agentic search across third-party apps (Slack, Google Drive, Salesforce, GitHub) represent a genuine product differentiation play. **If your product lacks an equivalent agentic layer, you risk losing the narrative in every enterprise evaluation.** Prioritize shipping and positioning your own AI automation/agent capabilities, even if limited. Create content and demos that show your AI value in the specific workflows Notion is targeting (filing tasks, writing reports, answering knowledge base questions). If you can't match the breadth, go deeper on specific use cases where your data advantage or integration maturity gives you an edge.

**6. Monitor and counter the emerging partner ecosystem before it scales.**
Notion is investing heavily in a consultant/agency ecosystem (Jobs 85, 89, 122, 148) with explicit goals to support *"thousands of partners globally"* and build a *"Black Belt Program"* for certified partner SEs. **This is a classic SaaS platform play—once the ecosystem takes hold, switching costs compound dramatically.** If you have an existing partner/consultant ecosystem, proactively engage the Notion consultants and agencies being recruited (many will be multi-vendor) with better economics, co-marketing, and certification programs. If you don't have a partner program, build one fast—particularly in EMEA and APAC where Notion's partner infrastructure is nascent. The Partner Strategy & Operations Manager role (Job 89) describes investment in *"tiering, processes, systems, and incentive models"*—match or exceed these incentives before they become the default.
