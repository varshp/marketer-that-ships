## Video: But what is a neural network? | Chapter 1, Deep learning

**TL;DR:** Neural networks learn to recognize patterns by iteratively adjusting millions of numerical weights across interconnected layers — no hand-coded rules required.

**Key takeaways:**
1. A neural network is a series of layers where each "neuron" holds a number between 0 and 1; the values in each layer are calculated from the previous layer using weighted connections and a bias, then squished through an activation function.
2. Training a network means finding the right weights and biases through exposure to thousands of labeled examples — the network learns by comparing its guesses to correct answers and nudging its parameters to do better next time.
3. Even a relatively small network (784 → 16 → 16 → 10 neurons) trained on handwritten digit images can achieve over 98% accuracy, demonstrating that neural networks are a practical, accessible entry point for teams new to machine learning.

**Action for B2B marketers:**
Identify one repetitive classification task your team does manually today — lead scoring, support ticket routing, or content tagging — and scope a 4-week pilot: collect 500–1,000 labeled examples, train a simple classifier using a free framework like scikit-learn or fast.ai, and present the accuracy results to leadership as a business case for broader ML investment.
