## G2 Review Synthesis: Stackly
*47 reviews analysed · B2B project management software · mid-market segment*

### Who is leaving reviews
Reviewers identify as: Operations Manager, Head of PMO, VP of Engineering, Project Manager, Director of Product, IT Manager. Most common company size: 51–500 employees. Industries: SaaS, professional services, e-commerce, agencies.

### Top 5 recurring complaints
1. **Onboarding is deceptively slow despite "5-minute setup" claims** — Reviewers report that connecting data sources and configuring automations actually takes 2–3 weeks for teams with existing tool stacks. "It looked like a weekend project. It became a Q3 initiative." (cited in 8 of 47 reviews)
2. **AI summaries are confident but frequently wrong** — Users flag that Stackly's AI generates project status summaries that sound authoritative but misrepresent actual task states, leading to embarrassing exec check-ins. "My VP read the AI summary in the standup. None of it was accurate." (cited in 11 reviews)
3. **No native JIRA or GitHub sync — the workarounds break constantly** — Engineering teams integrating Stackly alongside developer tooling describe the Zapier-based bridge as unreliable. "Every sprint we spend an hour debugging why the sync stopped again." (cited in 9 reviews)
4. **Permissions model is binary — admin or viewer, nothing in between** — Larger teams hit a wall where they need read-only access for stakeholders and edit access for contributors, but Stackly has no middle tier. (cited in 7 reviews)
5. **Mobile app is desktop-in-miniature, not designed for mobile** — Field teams and managers trying to approve work from their phones describe the experience as "zooming into a spreadsheet." (cited in 6 reviews)

### Top 5 recurring praise points
1. **Single pane of glass across all your projects — finally** — Teams consolidating from Asana + Confluence + spreadsheets describe immediate relief. "I opened Stackly on Monday morning and saw every project my team owned in one view. I hadn't had that in three years." (cited in 14 reviews)
2. **Gantt and timeline views are genuinely good** — Project managers with PM tool backgrounds consistently note that Stackly's timeline view is among the best they've used. "The dependency logic is smarter than anything in Monday or Asana at this price point." (cited in 10 reviews)
3. **Setup for simple projects is genuinely fast** — For small teams with low integration complexity, the product delivers on its speed promise. "Had my team running in 90 minutes. No IT, no training doc." (cited in 9 reviews)
4. **Budget-to-task linking is a hidden gem** — Finance-adjacent teams love that Stackly lets them attach budget line items to projects and see burn-rate in real time. (cited in 7 reviews)
5. **Customer support is unusually responsive** — Multiple reviewers describe getting responses from the founders themselves. "Got a DM from their CEO after I left a critical review. He fixed the issue in 48 hours." (cited in 8 reviews)
