## 10-K / Annual Report Summary: Stackly (FY2025)

### How they describe their customers (direct quotes from filing)

**From Business Overview:**
> "Our customers are primarily mid-market organizations with 50 to 500 employees, where operational complexity has outpaced the capabilities of spreadsheet-based coordination but where the cost and implementation burden of enterprise project management systems remains prohibitive."

> "The primary economic buyer is typically a Head of Operations, VP of Operations, or Director of PMO who owns cross-functional delivery and is accountable to executive leadership for project visibility and resource utilization."

> "We have observed that initial adoption frequently begins within a single operations team or department, led by an individual contributor or manager seeking immediate relief from manual status reporting. Expansion to adjacent teams and company-wide deployment is typically triggered when executive stakeholders encounter the product through a shared dashboard or board presentation."

**From Risk Factors:**
> "Our revenue depends substantially on our ability to retain and expand within existing customer accounts. Our net revenue retention rate was 118% for FY2025, driven primarily by seat expansion following internal advocacy from initial power users."

> "A significant portion of our sales cycles involve multiple stakeholders including IT, Finance, and Legal, each of whom may impose requirements that delay or prevent purchase. In particular, enterprise security requirements including SSO, SCIM provisioning, and audit logging have become increasingly common prerequisites at accounts with more than 200 employees."

**From Management Discussion:**
> "In FY2025, our average contract value increased 34% year-over-year, reflecting our success in moving upmarket toward companies with 200 to 500 employees and expanding initial deployments from team-level to department or company-wide deployments."

> "Customer acquisition costs remain elevated due to the multi-stakeholder nature of our sales cycles. A typical mid-market deal involves 3 to 5 stakeholders and a sales cycle of 45 to 90 days."
